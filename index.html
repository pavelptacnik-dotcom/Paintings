Pavel Ptáčník

Pavel Ptáčník (*1984, Kolín) is a Czech painter living and working in Prague. He studied textile design and subsequently graduated from the Figurative Painting Studio led by Daniel Balabán at the Faculty of Fine Arts, University of Ostrava. Alongside his painting practice, he works as a film concept artist and illustrator.

ARTIST STATEMENT

I do not perceive the human body as a stable object or as the bearer of a clearly defined identity. Rather, I see it as a temporary configuration of forces, emotions, biological processes, memories and experiences.

For me, the figure is not a portrait of a specific person. It carries traces of time, psychology, movement and relationships with the surrounding world. It is a place where these layers meet and influence one another.

I am interested in the moment when a being ceases to be merely a recognisable figure and begins to function as part of a wider system or situation, when the boundary between the individual and the environment becomes less distinct. The figure shifts from an object of observation into a field of possibilities, tension and meaning.

My paintings do not emerge from the observation of a single situation. They are the result of a prolonged process of assembling, combining and transforming visual sources. I work with photographs, digital studies, random associations and contemporary imaging technologies. Individual fragments lose their original meaning and enter new relationships. The resulting image is not a record of an actual event, but the construction of a new reality.

I am interested in the feeling that a viewer has seen a scene before, although it has never existed. I consider this paradox between the familiar and the unknown one of painting's most powerful tools.

I do not regard an image as an illustration of a previously formulated idea. Painting is not a means of explaining the world, but a way of exploring it. An image should be capable of affecting the viewer before they begin searching for its meaning.

I do not seek unambiguous answers or closed meanings. I am interested in a state of openness, uncertainty and possibility.
